Visit

The rooms are open from late afternoon until the last session ends. Admission is by reservation, twelve to a session, so that the instruments can be handled rather than watched.

A visit runs about ninety minutes and follows the collection in the order it was made. The attendant begins at the engraver's bench, works through the plates on the wall, and finishes at the north wall with the planisphere turning. Questions are welcome throughout; nothing on the wall is behind glass.

Sessions are held in the Long Room and the Print Study Room alternately, and the ticket states which door to use. Both rooms sit on the ground floor and are reached from Observatory Passage without steps.

Group visits

Schools, drawing groups and astronomical societies can book the Long Room for a private session on a Tuesday or Thursday afternoon. A group visit runs two hours and includes the instrument cases opened on the bench. Write to the desk with the size of the party and two dates that would suit.

Access

  • Step-free entry from Observatory Passage to both rooms.
  • Two accessible places per session; note this when reserving.
  • Seating is placed along the wall and can be moved on request.
  • Large-print copies of every caption are kept at the desk.
