Programme

The rooms stay open late on session evenings. Each viewing runs about ninety minutes, with the planisphere turning on the north wall and the instruments out of their cases.

Session notes

Every session is led by an attendant from the society and, on the demonstration evenings, by a maker from the bench. The pattern does not change much: the instruments come out of their cases first, on the bench where they can be picked up, and the plates are read after.

Because the plates are shown under raking light, the Long Room is kept dim. Visitors who would rather see a plate flat and lit are asked to say so at the desk and the attendant will bring it to the bench at the end of the session.

A session can be rearranged up to the day before. Cancel in writing and the place returns to the waitlist the same evening, which is how most of the full evenings fill up again.
