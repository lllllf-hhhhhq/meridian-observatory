Reference catalogues

The measured positions quoted on the planisphere and the Plates page come from published catalogues rather than from the engraving.

  • Star positions, visual magnitudes and spectral types follow the Yale Bright Star Catalog as catalogued in VizieR V/50, quoted from the corresponding SIMBAD records.
  • Deep sky object coordinates, types and magnitudes are quoted from SIMBAD.
  • Galaxy distances follow NASA/IPAC NED.

Typefaces
